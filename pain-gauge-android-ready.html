About this tool

This pain-tracking tool was developed by Dr. Zainab Khan, who holds a PhD in Geography and whose research spans environmental monitoring, climate change, spatial analysis, and geospatial research.

Beyond her academic research, she has a strong interest in science, coding, problem-solving, and developing practical digital tools. This scientific and problem-solving mindset, together with her own experience of living with long-term musculoskeletal and inflammatory pain, inspired her to develop this tool. It is designed to help people living with autoimmune, inflammatory, and arthritic conditions document where pain occurs, monitor changes over time, and maintain a structured record that can be exported or shared when needed.

The tool is intended to support personal symptom documentation and communication. It is not intended to diagnose disease or replace professional medical advice.

Pain Gauge stores records locally on this device. You can export, share, or create a portable backup of your records.
