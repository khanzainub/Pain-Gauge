Pain Gauge · AIIMS version privacy

Updated 17 October 2026. This independent tool is created by Zainab Khan; its folder name does not imply an institutional AIIMS affiliation.

What is recorded

You choose your health profile, name, condition, medicines, diet, pain locations and scores, sleep, mood and optional period information. A stable patient ID is derived from your Google account ID. Your verified Google email and account ID link the diary to your account.

Google sign-in and Drive

Google sign-in is required before the AIIMS diary or care-team dashboard opens. English and Hindi are available on the sign-in page. Signing out or an expired Google session closes the interface without deleting your local records or Drive backups.

Google handles authentication and permission. Pain Gauge does not receive or store your Google password. Temporary OAuth tokens remain in page memory and disappear on sign-out or reload; you may need to reconnect. Once you approve Drive access, saved information syncs from your browser to a Pain Gauge folder in your own Google Drive while the page is open and the session is valid.

The patient view requests drive.file for its app-accessible files and drive.appdata for private backup/restore. It does not request Gmail access. The admin separately grants drive.readonly so the dashboard can discover and read patient folders shared with that account. That Google scope can technically read other files available to the admin; this implementation only queries Pain Gauge patient folders and records. Google may require additional consent or app verification.

Admin sharing

Sharing is optional. When you enable “Allow my care team to view”, all doctors in the registered care team receive viewer permission on your patient folder. The permission includes doctors registered in future. You can expand “Who can view my diary?” to inspect the current names and Google accounts. Initially the team contains Zainab Khan. All information in that folder, including sleep, mood and period entries, becomes visible to those doctors. Newly registered doctors are granted access when this connected diary next syncs; while open and authorized, it also checks team changes every minute. Existing individual-doctor permission is not treated as consent to share with future team members. The app requests no email notification. Other patients cannot access it through this app. Google Drive permissions enforce actual access; knowing a patient ID is not sufficient.

Turn sharing off to remove the app-managed care-team permissions from the folder. An earlier individual-doctor share can be removed separately without enabling team sharing. Changes cannot reach a patient’s Drive while that patient is offline or their Google session has expired. Sign-out alone does not remove sharing. Direct shares you create separately in Drive, downloaded copies, screenshots or prior exports cannot be withdrawn by the app. The dashboard refreshes while open; it is not guaranteed continuous clinical observation or an emergency service.

Local storage, backup and restore

AIIMS records are stored under separate local keys for each signed-in account. Signing out switches away from that account’s diary; it does not delete its local copies. This is not encryption: somebody with access to your browser/device storage may still read them. Use a private device. There is no separate central patient database.

Back up now saves a separate AIIMS private backup. Restore asks before replacing the local diary and keeps up to five local recovery copies. Older original Pain Gauge backups can be read for migration; this extension does not overwrite them or the root app’s local records. A different existing cloud diary pauses auto-sync until you restore or explicitly choose this device’s version. Cloud recovery files are made before explicit replacement of a different saved backup. Your exported CSV/Excel includes the new health fields.

Services and retention

GitHub Pages hosts the code. Google handles identity and stores your Drive records. Network requests disclose ordinary connection information to those providers. Health records are not committed to GitHub, sold, used for advertising or sent for AI training by this tool. Local records remain until you delete them or clear browser storage. Drive files, private recovery backups and permissions remain until you remove them. Keep independent exports; uninterrupted storage, syncing and monitoring are not guaranteed.

गोपनीयता · मुख्य बातें

Google से अनुमति देने के बाद आपकी सेव की हुई जानकारी आपके अपने Drive folder में अपडेट होती है। आपका Google पासवर्ड इस ऐप को नहीं मिलता। कनेक्शन समाप्त होने या पेज दोबारा खोलने पर फिर से Google से जुड़ना पड़ सकता है।

Care-team sharing चालू करने पर टीम में दर्ज सभी doctors—और भविष्य में जोड़े गए doctors—आपके folder की पूरी जानकारी देख सकते हैं—खान-पान, दवाइयाँ, दर्द, नींद, मनोदशा और माहवारी सहित। नए doctor को अनुमति आपके अगले connected sync पर मिलती है। पहले की सिर्फ एक doctor वाली अनुमति अपने-आप पूरी team की सहमति नहीं मानी जाती। Sharing बंद करने से app द्वारा दी गई team की अनुमति हटती है; पहले डाउनलोड की गई कॉपी वापस नहीं ली जा सकती। केवल sign out करने से sharing बंद नहीं होती।

अलग Google accounts की स्थानीय जानकारी अलग रखी जाती है, लेकिन डिवाइस का storage पढ़ सकने वाला व्यक्ति इसे देख सकता है। पुराने original Pain Gauge का डेटा नहीं बदला जाता। Backup और Restore उपलब्ध हैं; रिकॉर्ड बदलने से पहले पुष्टि माँगी जाती है। यह लगातार डॉक्टर की निगरानी या आपातकालीन सेवा नहीं है।
