About this tool

Pain Gauge was created by Zainab Khan. Living with long-term pain and the exhaustion of repeatedly trying to describe it inspired her to build a clearer, practical way to record symptoms.

Pain Gauge is a pain diary for recording pain locations, intensity, medication and diet, and reviewing changes through charts. It supports public wellbeing; it does not diagnose conditions and is not a substitute for medical advice.

Use Pain Gauge in your web browser and bookmark this link. Your records stay in this browser, but clearing its data, reinstalling the browser or losing the device can remove them. For long-term storage, regularly back up to your own Google Drive and keep downloaded CSV copies.

If you need to reconnect to Google Drive, your saved backup remains there. On a new device or an empty browser, restore your existing backup before making a new one.

Your health records are not used for advertising, sale or AI training. They are not sent automatically to Zainab Khan; she can see them only if you choose to share them.

Please keep your own copies. Data retention is not guaranteed, and the creator does not accept responsibility for data loss.
